# Where to Buy What

Store-agnostic: map these categories onto whatever the planner has (profile
`stores` block). Pack sizes and prices drift by chain and region, so treat
these as starting points and verify before finalizing.

## Warehouse club (Costco, Sam's Club, BJ's, …): bulk staples

| Item | Typical pack | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Sandwich bread | 2-loaf pack (~20–24 oz each) | Store brand is fine |
| Peanut butter | 2 × 40–48 oz jars | Creamy; kids prefer it |
| Jelly | 2–3 lb jars or 2-packs | Grape is the kid default |
| Deli turkey (sliced) | 1.5–2 lb packs | Covers no-beef/no-pork; **not** halal/kosher-certified |
| Sliced cheese | 2–2.5 lb packs (~60–80 slices) | Pick kosher-certified if halal/kosher is enabled |
| Hummus | ~32 oz tubs | Veg sandwich base |
| Lettuce, pre-washed / ready-to-eat | Large bags or clamshells | No rinsing needed at camp |
| Chip variety packs | 30–50 bags | Check flavors if nut-free |
| Clementines / bananas / apples | 3–5 lb bags / bunches | Zero prep |
| Bottled water | 35–40 packs | |
| Plates, napkins, gloves, zip bags | Bulk | Often already in the group's supply bin; ask |
| Condiment packets | 100–200 count | Stock varies by club; the grocery store is the fallback |

With several memberships, choose **one** club per trip:
- Compare total price for the whole list, not item by item.
- Consider pickup/ordering convenience; some clubs show pickup prices online.
- Online prices can differ from in-warehouse prices, and third-party
  same-day delivery is usually marked up.

## Grocery store (Kroger family, Walmart, H-E-B, Publix, Meijer, Safeway, …)

- SunButter and nut-free labeled snacks
- Gluten-free bread (often the freezer section)
- Kosher deli meat (kosher section, where stocked); halal where stocked
- Small quantities, when a warehouse pack would be mostly waste
- Last-minute top-ups the morning of
- Set the preferred store on the website first so prices and stock are local

## Specialty (only if a requirement needs it)

- Halal grocer or butcher for certified halal deli meat; call ahead for sliced turkey
- Kosher deli or market for certified meat or sealed prepackaged sandwiches

## Price-check notes (learned 2026-09)
- **Sam's Club**: nearly the whole list is searchable online with club pickup
  prices, including bread, produce and condiment packets. Easiest to price and
  pre-order.
- **Costco**: the website omits most fresh and warehouse-only items (bread,
  jelly, produce, condiment packets), so a full-list comparison isn't possible
  online. Only compare the items it does list; flag the rest as unpriced.
- Certified deli meat (halal/kosher) generally isn't listed at warehouse clubs.
  Grocery chain sites may not list it either; tell the planner to call ahead.
- Watch for tree nuts in "variety" items (e.g. pine-nut hummus) when nut-free
  status is unknown.
