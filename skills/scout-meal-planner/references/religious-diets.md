# Religious Diet Rules (load only when halal or kosher is enabled)

Each rule applies only when its requirement is `"enabled": true` in the event
profile. If neither is on, skip this file entirely.

## Halal (when enabled)
- Certified halal meat only (halal grocer or butcher; some grocery chains stock it; call ahead).
- Cheese must use microbial/vegetable enzymes, not animal rennet. Kosher-certified
  cheese satisfies this.
- Don't substitute kosher meat without asking the families; acceptance varies.
- Halal sandwiches may include cheese.

## Kosher (when enabled)
- Certified kosher meat, sealed pack (grocery kosher section or kosher deli).
- **No meat + dairy together**: kosher turkey sandwiches get no cheese.
- Every ingredient on a kosher sandwich is certified (OU, OK, Star-K, etc.):
  bread, mayo/mustard packets.
- Stricter families may not eat food prepared outside a kosher kitchen. Ask;
  if so, buy sealed, prepackaged sandwiches from a kosher deli instead.
- Buy kosher-certified versions of the **shared** items too (bread, PB, jelly,
  cheese, hummus, condiment packets). Most mainstream brands already are, so it
  costs nothing and makes PB&J and veg sandwiches usable for more people.

## Handling (either enabled)
- Pre-make certified sandwiches **first**, before any regular deli is opened:
  wiped surface, fresh gloves, new disposable knife. Kosher batch first, then halal.
- Seal and label each ("KOSHER — turkey, no dairy" / "HALAL"), store in their
  own cooler bag, serve from a labeled grab-and-go bin with an adult nearby.
  Never on the shared tong line.
- Keep the whole line pork-free.

## Related (not a toggle)
- No beef (often Hindu): regular turkey is usually fine. Confirm with families.
- If the no-pork group's reason is unknown, ask whether it's religious before
  enabling halal/kosher.

## Unknown counts
- Round the guess up and mark it `"estimated": true` in the profile.
- Buy one extra sealed certified pack as backup. It stays unopened and goes
  home if unused.
