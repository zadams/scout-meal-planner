Decisions built into the plan

Turkey + vegetarian at every meal
The 17 people with restrictions are vegetarian, no pork, or no red meat. Every meal has a turkey (non-red-meat) option and a vegetarian option, and there's no pork or beef anywhere.
Hummus and veggies
Lunch's vegetarian option is a hummus-and-veggie sandwich (cucumber, peppers, lettuce, tomato; cheese optional). Cucumbers and pepper strips are on the line for everyone.
Pork gelatin
Regular marshmallows are usually pork gelatin, so vegetarian, no-pork and no-red-meat eaters get vegan marshmallows from a separate, labeled tray.
Fruit
Bought together for breakfast and lunch by Group 3 (already in the Sam's cart), with ~83 servings handed to Group 2. Group 2 doesn't buy fruit. Sunday's fruit comes from what's left.
Oil
One bottle of vegetable oil covers the breakfast griddle and dinner. No separate cooking spray.
Grilled cheese
Still off at lunch: it would need a second hot station and a batch cook for 83. The hummus sandwich covers vegetarians.
S'mores
About 3.5 per kid each night, with +50% marshmallows for burning and dropping. That allowance counts as used.
Halal and kosher
Off. No family has asked for certified meat. Turning either back on adds certified turkey and sealed meals automatically.
Saturday coffee
Coffee, tea and hot chocolate at Saturday breakfast were added by the planner; the scoutmaster listed coffee only for Sunday.
Mess kits
Campers bring their own, so no plates, bowls or cups are bought. Napkins, 12 rolls of paper towels and crew-sized gloves (adult and kid) are included.

Countdown (T = serving time)

  1. T-45Start the fire early; roasting works best over coals, not tall flames. Keep a bucket of water next to the fire ring.
  2. T-30Heat water in pots (about 1 gallon per 16 cups). Whisk the mix into the big Igloo with the hot water (53 servings for this meal).
  3. T-15Trays: break graham crackers (215 sheets) into squares; break chocolate bars (72 bars) into squares. Set out the pack's reusable skewers.
  4. T-10Marshmallows: regular (173 mallows) on one tray.
  5. T-10Vegan marshmallows (43 mallows) go on their OWN labeled tray, with their own skewers if possible (regular marshmallows are pork gelatin).
  • Hand out marshmallows 2 at a time; they'll burn and drop a lot, and that's planned for.
  • Kids assemble on a napkin or paper towel away from the fire.
  • About 3-4 per kid over the night; slow it down if the trays empty too fast.
  • Serve from the Igloo spigot. Top off with cool water for younger kids so it isn't scalding.

Crew

  • Fire lead (1 adult): fire, and the roasting line
  • Station lead (1 adult) + AOL scouts: trays, handing out marshmallows, hot chocolate

  • pack's reusable roasting skewers (bring them; no purchase)

Breakfast

Egg breakfast burritos + fruit + Coffee + tea (packets) + Hot chocolate (bulk, in the big Igloo) · planned for 83 · crew 4 adults, 4 kids

VegetarianEverything on the line is vegetarian (no meat at this meal).
No porkOK as served: no meat at this meal.
No red meatOK as served: no meat at this meal.

Countdown (T = serving time)

  1. T-60Handwash station up (water jug, soap, paper towels). Wipe down prep tables. Set up griddles and burners; check fuel.
  2. T-45Adults: dice 3 onions and 3 peppers on cutting boards. Kids: wash hands, glove up, and set out whole fruit (83 servings) in bins.
  3. T-40Crack 275 eggs into large bowls, about 5 dozen per bowl. Whisk in a little salt and pepper. Keep the bowls in the cooler until they go on the griddle.
  4. T-30Griddles to medium. Melt a little butter, then saute the peppers and onions 5 minutes until soft. Move them to a covered pan.
  5. T-30Heat water in pots (about 1 gallon per 16 cups). Whisk the mix into the big Igloo with the hot water (53 servings for this meal).
  6. T-25Scramble the eggs in batches (about 2 dozen per griddle at a time) with butter. Cook until firm with no runny parts. Hold in covered foil pans at the cool edge of the griddle.
  7. T-20Heat water in a large kettle or pot. Set out the coffee packets (65 packets), tea bags, creamer, sugar and sweetener with stir sticks.
  8. T-10Warm the tortillas (138 tortillas) 15-20 seconds per side; stack them in foil to stay soft.
  9. T-5Set out the line: tortillas, eggs, peppers & onions, shredded cheese (73.0 oz), salsa (50.2 oz), hot sauce, fruit, napkins.

Serving line

plates (or mess kits) → tortillas → eggs (spoon) → peppers & onions → cheese → salsa → hot sauce → fruit → napkins

  • One burrito each to start; seconds once everyone has gone through.
  • About 1/3 cup of eggs per burrito. Adults serve the eggs; kids can serve cheese and fruit.
  • Hot sauce stays at the end of the line, away from little hands.
  • Adults serve themselves; hot water stays on the adults' end of the table.
  • Serve from the Igloo spigot. Top off with cool water for younger kids so it isn't scalding.

Crew

  • Griddle leads (2 adults): veggies, eggs, tortillas
  • Prep (1 adult): crack and whisk eggs, dice veggies
  • Line lead (1 adult) + kid helpers: fruit, setting out the line, serving cheese and salsa

Equipment

  • griddles
  • burners
  • spatulas
  • large bowls + whisks
  • cutting boards + knives
  • foil or a covered pan for holding
  • burner + large kettle or pot for hot water
  • large Igloo beverage cooler for hot chocolate
  • burners + large pots to heat water

Food safety

  • Raw eggs stay in the cooler until they hit the griddle. Separate spoons for raw and cooked eggs.
  • Cook eggs until firm (160°F). Cooked eggs are out no more than 2 hours (1 hour above 90°F).
  • Change gloves after handling raw eggs or shells.
  • Hot water is adults only.
  • Test the temperature before kids drink it.

Cleanup and leftovers

  • Scrape griddles while warm. Egg shells and food scraps go in the trash, not the fire (critters).
  • Leftover cooked eggs are thrown out, not saved.
  • Bag and label opened tortillas, cheese, and leftover diced veggies (see carry-forwards).
  • Rinse the Igloo while warm.
  • Save leftover onions for sat-lunch
  • Save leftover bell peppers for sat-lunch
  • Save leftover whole fruit for sat-lunch

Lunch

Sandwich lunch (PB&J + build-your-own deli) + chips + fruit + Lemonade / Kool-Aid (bulk mix) · planned for 83 · crew 5 adults, 6 kids

VegetarianHummus + veggie sandwich (cucumber, peppers, lettuce, tomato; cheese optional) on the build-your-own line. A full allotment is planned for them.
No porkTurkey sandwich on the build-your-own line (no pork anywhere at this meal).
No red meatTurkey sandwich on the build-your-own line (no beef or pork anywhere at this meal).

Countdown (T = serving time)

  1. T-60Handwash station up. Set up tables and trash. Wipe down two prep surfaces: a small one for certified sandwiches, a long one for PB&J.
  2. T-45PB&J assembly line: lay out bread → peanut butter → jelly → close → cut in half. Make 75 sandwiches using about 93.6 oz of peanut butter and 59.9 oz of jelly. Halves go on foil trays; cover them.
  3. T-35Toppings: on the second prep surface (after any certified sandwiches are sealed and away), slice tomatoes, cucumbers and red onions thin and cut peppers into strips. Covered pans, into the cooler.
  4. T-15Fill the 5-gal coolers with cold water and ice. Stir in the mix: one canister makes about 34 qt, so about half a canister per 5-gal cooler (145 servings for this meal). Keep one cooler of plain water too.
  5. T-10Build-your-own line: bread, hummus in a bowl with its own spoon (veggies next to it), one tray each of turkey (7.7 lb total) and cheese, then toppings (each with its own tongs or fork), then mayo/mustard packets. Keep backup trays in the cooler.

Serving line

hand sanitizer → grab-and-go bins: PB&J · HALAL · KOSHER → bread → hummus (own spoon) → turkey (tongs) → cheese (tongs) → toppings → mayo/mustard packets → chips → fruit → napkins

  • One sandwich to start (PB&J halves count as one); seconds once everyone has gone through.
  • An adult stands by the certified bins and hands those sandwiches to the right families.
  • One chip bag and one piece of fruit each to start.
  • Serve from the spigot into mess-kit cups.

Crew

  • Certified sandwiches (1 adult): kosher, then halal, before anything else is opened; then slices tomatoes/onions
  • PB&J assembly line (3 adults + scouts): bread → PB → jelly → close → cut
  • Line lead (1 adult): sets out the build-your-own line, refills from the cooler

Equipment

  • 2 prep tables
  • foil pans + lids
  • tongs
  • bowls for toppings
  • 2 x 5-gal drink coolers with spigots
  • potable water

Food safety

  • Turkey, cheese, hummus and cut toppings stay in the cooler (40°F or colder) until serving; out no more than 2 hours (1 hour above 90°F).
  • Put out one tray at a time and refill from the cooler rather than setting everything out.
  • Gloves change between the certified batches and regular food, and after touching anything else.

Cleanup and leftovers

  • Bag and label opened turkey, cheese, bread and cut toppings; back in the cooler (see carry-forwards).
  • Wipe tables; pack out trash.
  • Rinse the coolers; drain away from the campsite.
  • Save leftover deli turkey, sliced for sat-dinner (use with the ground turkey)
  • Save leftover lettuce, pre-washed for sat-dinner (use with the salad greens, pre-washed)
  • Save leftover tomatoes for sat-dinner (use with the salad greens, pre-washed)
  • Save leftover onions for sat-dinner
  • Save leftover cucumbers for sat-dinner (use with the salad greens, pre-washed)
  • Save leftover bell peppers for sat-dinner (use with the salad greens, pre-washed)
  • Save leftover whole fruit for sun-breakfast

Dinner

Pasta with meat sauce and vegetarian sauce + small salad + Lemonade / Kool-Aid (bulk mix) · planned for 83 · crew 4 adults, 6 kids

VegetarianVEG sauce pot (own ladle) + pasta + salad.
No porkMEAT sauce is ground turkey (no pork), or the VEG sauce.
No red meatMEAT sauce is ground turkey (no beef or pork), or the VEG sauce.

Countdown (T = serving time)

  1. T-75Fill 2-3 large pots with water (about 5 quarts per pound of pasta, cooked in batches). Cover and bring to a boil; camp burners take 30-45 minutes.
  2. T-60Handwash station up. Wipe down prep tables. Dice 2 onions for the meat sauce.
  3. T-50VEG SAUCE FIRST: in its own pot, warm about 40% of the marinara (286.1 oz total) with Italian seasoning and garlic powder. Label the pot VEG and give it its own ladle.
  4. T-45MEAT SAUCE: brown the ground turkey (7.2 lb) in a little oil with the diced onion, salt and pepper until there's no pink (165°F). Drain, add the rest of the marinara plus seasoning, and simmer 15 minutes. Label the pot MEAT.
  5. T-30Salt the boiling water (about 14.6 oz across all batches). Cook the pasta (319.2 oz) in batches of about 2 lb per pot, 9-11 minutes. Drain, toss with a little oil, and hold covered.
  6. T-15Fill the 5-gal coolers with cold water and ice. Stir in the mix: one canister makes about 34 qt, so about half a canister per 5-gal cooler (145 servings for this meal). Keep one cooler of plain water too.
  7. T-10Salad: greens (47.2 oz) into bowls; dressing (26.1 oz) on the side. Parmesan (14.6 oz) in a bowl with a spoon.

Serving line

plates/bowls (or mess kits) → pasta → VEG sauce (own ladle) → MEAT sauce (own ladle) → parmesan → salad + dressing → napkins

  • Line order: mess kits → pasta → VEG sauce → MEAT sauce → parmesan → salad. The veg sauce comes first so the meat ladle never touches it.
  • About 1 cup of pasta to start (1.5 for adults); seconds once everyone has gone through.
  • Keep both sauces on low heat, lids on.
  • Serve from the spigot into mess-kit cups.

Crew

  • Water + pasta (2 adults): boiling water and draining are adults only
  • Sauces (1 adult): veg pot first, then meat pot
  • Salad + line (1 adult) + AOL scouts: salad bowls, serving parmesan and salad, cleanup

Equipment

  • 2-3 large stock pots for pasta
  • 2 pots for sauce (meat and veg, kept separate)
  • burners
  • colander
  • ladles
  • tongs
  • 2 x 5-gal drink coolers with spigots
  • potable water

Food safety

  • Ground turkey is cooked to 165°F with no pink.
  • Only adults handle boiling water and draining. Kids stay back from the burners.
  • Hot food stays hot; leftovers go into shallow containers and into the cooler within 2 hours.

Cleanup and leftovers

  • Let pasta water cool, strain out the food, and dispose of it per campground rules, away from water sources.
  • Scrape pots before washing. Sauce leftovers go home in containers; nothing is cooked Sunday.
  • Bag unused salad greens.
  • Rinse the coolers; drain away from the campsite.
