## Meals

### Fri S'mores
*S'mores* · **Group 1 – AOLs** · planned for ~83
- Kids go wild: planned ~3.5 s'mores per kid, ~1.5 per adult, per night.
- Marshmallows carry a +50% burn/drop allowance; grahams +15% for breakage.
- Vegan (gelatin-free) marshmallows go in a separate, labeled bag for vegetarian, halal and kosher eaters. Regular marshmallows are usually pork gelatin.
- Equipment: campfire, roasting sticks, wet wipes
- Prep T-15: Open graham boxes and break bars into squares on a tray; set out both marshmallow bags, the vegan one labeled.
- Prep T-0: An adult runs the fire line; hand out marshmallows 2 at a time so bags don't disappear into the fire.
- Uses: graham crackers 215 sheets; marshmallows (regular) 170 mallows; vegan marshmallows (gelatin-free, kosher) 45 mallows; milk chocolate bars (1.55 oz) 72 bars; napkins 123 napkins

### Sat Breakfast
*Egg breakfast burritos + fruit* · **Group 2 – Lions/Tigers/Wolves** · planned for ~80
- "Lots of eggs" per the scoutmaster: planned 2.5 eggs per kid, 3.5 per adult.
- Build-your-own: scrambled eggs, shredded cheese, peppers/onions, salsa. No meat, so it is pork- and beef-free and works for vegetarians.
- Ask about egg allergies; a scout with one needs a separate plan.
- 3 kosher eater(s) get a sealed certified meal; hot food from shared pots/griddles usually isn't kosher. Ask the families.
- Equipment: griddles, burners, spatulas, large bowls + whisks, cutting boards + knives, foil or a covered pan for holding
- Line: plates → tortillas → eggs (spoon) → peppers & onions → cheese → salsa → fruit → napkins
- Prep T-45: Dice onions and peppers; crack and whisk eggs in batches of ~5 dozen.
- Prep T-30: Griddles on; saute peppers/onions first, then scramble eggs in batches into a covered pan.
- Prep T-10: Warm tortillas on the griddle edge; set out cheese, salsa, fruit.
- Uses: eggs 265 eggs; burrito tortillas (10") 133 tortillas; shredded cheese 70.4 oz; onions 3 onions; bell peppers 3 peppers; salsa 48.4 oz; cooking spray / oil 2 cans; whole fruit 80 servings; plates 80 plates; napkins 160 napkins; sealed kosher-certified meal (hot-meal alternative) 3 meals

### Sat Dinner
*Pasta with meat sauce and vegetarian sauce + small salad* · **Group 4 – AOLs** · planned for ~80
- Meat sauce uses ground turkey, not beef, so no-beef families are covered. It is not halal or kosher.
- Vegetarian sauce is the planned main for vegetarian, halal and kosher eaters, plus ~25% of everyone else.
- Separate ladles for each sauce; label the pots. Cook the veg sauce first.
- Salad is intentionally small: pre-washed greens, one or two dressings.
- 3 kosher eater(s) get a sealed certified meal; hot food from shared pots/griddles usually isn't kosher. Ask the families.
- Equipment: 2-3 large stock pots for pasta, 2 pots for sauce (meat and veg, kept separate), burners, colander, ladles, tongs
- Line: plates/bowls → pasta → VEG sauce (own ladle) → MEAT sauce (own ladle) → parmesan → salad + dressing → napkins
- Prep T-60: Water on to boil (large pots take 30+ min on camp burners).
- Prep T-45: Veg sauce: warm marinara in its own pot. Meat sauce: brown turkey with diced onion, add marinara, simmer.
- Prep T-20: Cook pasta in batches; toss with a little oil so it doesn't clump.
- Prep T-5: Salad greens into bowls, dressing on the side.
- Uses: pasta, dry 308.6 oz; marinara sauce, jarred 281.7 oz; ground turkey 6.4 lb; onions 2 onions; grated parmesan 14.1 oz; salad greens, pre-washed 45.5 oz; salad dressing 25.2 oz; plates 80 plates; napkins 160 napkins; sealed kosher-certified meal (hot-meal alternative) 3 meals

## Label checks
- Kosher enabled: look for a kosher symbol (OU, OK, Star-K, ...). → Graham crackers, Milk chocolate bars (1.55 oz), Burrito tortillas (10"), Shredded cheese, Salsa, Sandwich bread, Peanut butter, Grape jelly, Sliced cheese, Hummus (plain), Dill pickle chips, Mayo packets, Mustard packets, Pasta, dry, Marinara sauce, jarred, Grated parmesan, Salad dressing, Breakfast bars
- Regular gelatin is usually pork-derived: not OK for vegetarian, halal, kosher, or no-pork eaters. → Marshmallows (regular), Breakfast bars
- Nut status unknown or nut-free: check for nut / tree-nut ingredients and 'may contain' warnings. → Milk chocolate bars (1.55 oz), Hummus (plain), Chips, snack bags, Breakfast bars
