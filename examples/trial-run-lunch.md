# Trial Run Lunch — Sandwiches, Chips & Fruit

**Headcount:** 73 confirmed = 35 kids/scouts + 38 adults, **plus a buffer of 10 walk-ups (planning for 83)**.
The walk-ups are covered with extra PB&J, chips and fruit, all shelf-stable. They don't add to the turkey order, so nothing gets thrown out if they don't come.
**Also planned:** 8 very hungry guests eating about 1.5 extra sandwiches each, plus a 5% allowance for prep accidents (dropped slices, squished loaves, torn bread) on bread, spreads, turkey and cheese.
**Estimated counts** (no RSVP data): 10 vegetarian, 5 halal, 3 kosher. These are guesses from the planner's memory of the last trip. Halal and kosher are rounded up because those families have no substitute on the line, and each has one sealed backup pack.
Inputs and requirement toggles: `examples/trial-run-lunch.json`. Recalculate: `python3 skills/scout-meal-planner/scripts/sandwich_lunch.py --profile examples/trial-run-lunch.json`

## Summary
- **All prep happens at camp, right before lunch.**
- **Grab-and-go:** PB&J halves on covered trays (made assembly-line style just before serving), plus sealed, labeled **halal** and **kosher** turkey sandwiches (made first).
- **Build-your-own line:** regular turkey sandwiches and vegetarian sandwiches (hummus + cheese), with **toppings: lettuce, tomato, pickle chips, red onion**, plus mayo/mustard packets.
- **Sides:** chips and whole fruit. The whole line is pork-free.

**No grilled cheese:** it needs a griddle, fuel and a volunteer cooking in batches for 83 people.
**Cost:** ≈ $290–315 total, about $3.50–3.80/person. Sam's Club items are priced online (2026-09-24); certified turkey and supplies are estimates.

### Why Sam's, not Costco
- Costco's website doesn't list most of this list (bread, jelly, produce, condiment packets), so it can't be priced or ordered for pickup. You'd be shopping blind in the warehouse.
- Where both could be compared, it's a wash. Costco's Hillshire turkey (33 oz for $8.89) would save about $18. Its 54-ct chip packs would cost about $10 more.
- Costco's hummus includes a **pine nut** variety. Pine nuts are a tree nut, so avoid it while nut allergies are unknown.

### Cheaper swaps if the budget is tight
- **Mayo and mustard in squeeze bottles** instead of packets: saves about $15, but it's slower and less sanitary on the line. The leftover packets would keep for future campouts.
- **Member's Mark Natural PB** (2 × 40 oz for $7.78) instead of Skippy: saves about $6. It separates and needs stirring, and kids tend to prefer regular.

## Prep timeline (all at camp, right before lunch)
**Crew:** 5 volunteers (1 for the certified sandwiches, then setup; 4 on PB&J).

| When | What |
|---|---|
| **T–60 min** | Set up tables, the handwash station and trash. Wipe down two prep surfaces: one small one for certified sandwiches, one long one for PB&J. |
| **T–50** | **Certified first**, before any regular turkey is opened. **Kosher:** fresh gloves, new disposable knife; ~6 sandwiches of kosher bread + kosher turkey + lettuce and pickles from **freshly opened** bag/jar + kosher mayo/mustard packets on the side, **no cheese**. Seal, label "KOSHER — turkey, no dairy", and put in their own cooler bag. **Halal:** wipe down, fresh gloves, new knife; ~9 sandwiches of halal turkey + cheese + lettuce and pickles. Seal, label "HALAL", and put in their own cooler bag. About 10–15 min. |
| **T–45** | **PB&J assembly line**, 4 people: lay out bread → PB → jelly → close and cut. ~75 sandwiches in ~30–40 min. Stack the halves on foil trays and cover them. |
| **T–35** | Setup person slices ~7 tomatoes, then 3 red onions thin, on the certified surface (after the certified sandwiches are sealed and away). About 15 min. Both go into covered pans in the cooler. |
| **T–10** | Put out the build-your-own line: bread, hummus in a bowl with its own spoon, one tray each of turkey and cheese, then the toppings (lettuce, tomato, pickles, onion), each in its own bowl with its own tongs or fork, then packets. Keep the backups in the cooler. |
| **T–0** | Serve. One adult watches the certified bin; one refills the turkey and cheese from the cooler. |

Tomatoes and onions are the only things cut at camp. Everything else is pre-washed, jarred, pre-sliced, whole or bagged.

## Service line
With 83 people, set up a **two-sided table**.
1. Hand sanitizer / handwash
2. Plates
3. **Grab-and-go bins:** PB&J · HALAL · KOSHER. The halal and kosher bins stay sealed and labeled, and an adult stands by them
4. Build-your-own: bread → hummus (own spoon) → turkey (tongs) → cheese (tongs) → **lettuce → tomato → onion → pickles** (each its own utensil) → mayo/mustard packets
5. Chips → fruit
6. Napkins → trash bag

Food safety: keep all turkey, cheese, hummus and sliced tomatoes/onions in the cooler at 40°F or colder. Don't leave them out more than 2 hours, or more than 1 hour above 90°F.

## Open questions
- **Real counts:** vegetarian, halal and kosher are all estimates. Add these questions (plus nut allergies) to the RSVP for next time. For kosher families, ask whether volunteer-made sandwiches are OK or whether they need sealed prepackaged sandwiches from a kosher deli.
- Are the no-beef families OK with regular (non-certified) turkey?
- Would the halal families accept kosher turkey? If so, one certified product covers both and simplifies shopping. Don't assume; ask.
- Are 8 big eaters, a 5% prep loss and 10 walk-ups about right?
- **Is a 5-person crew for the hour before lunch realistic?** With fewer people, start the PB&J earlier or make it build-your-own (slower line, messier).
- Drinks aren't in this plan. Is someone else covering them?
- **Where to get certified halal and kosher deli turkey locally.** Call ahead; neither is listed online at Sam's or the local Kroger.
