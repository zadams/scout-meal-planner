Fri S'mores

S'mores + Hot chocolate (bulk, in the big Igloo) · planned for about 83 people

Special diets at this meal

Vegetarian Vegan marshmallows from the labeled tray.
Halal Vegan marshmallows from the labeled tray.
Kosher Vegan (kosher-certified) marshmallows from the labeled tray; check the graham and chocolate symbols.

Crew

  • Fire lead (1 adult): fire, and the roasting line
  • Station lead (1 adult) + AOL scouts: trays, handing out marshmallows, hot chocolate

Equipment

  • campfire
  • pack's reusable roasting skewers (bring them; no purchase)
  • wet wipes
  • large Igloo beverage cooler for hot chocolate
  • burners + large pots to heat water

Food & supplies for this meal

Graham crackers215 sheets
Marshmallows (regular)170 mallows
Vegan marshmallows (gelatin-free, kosher)45 mallows
Milk chocolate bars (1.55 oz)72 bars
Napkins123 napkins
Paper towels2 rolls
Zip-top bags, gallon3 bags
Hot chocolate mix, bulk53 servings

Prep & cooking (T = serving time)

T-45Start the fire early; roasting works best over coals, not tall flames. Keep a bucket of water next to the fire ring.
T-30Heat water in pots (about 1 gallon per 16 cups). Whisk the mix into the big Igloo with the hot water (53 servings for this meal).
T-15Trays: break graham crackers (215 sheets) into squares; break chocolate bars (72 bars) into squares. Set out the pack's reusable skewers.
T-10Marshmallows: regular (170 mallows) on one tray.
T-10Vegan marshmallows (45 mallows) go on their OWN labeled tray, with their own skewers if possible (regular marshmallows are pork gelatin).

Serving

  • Hand out marshmallows 2 at a time; they'll burn and drop a lot, and that's planned for.
  • Kids assemble on a napkin or paper towel away from the fire.
  • About 3-4 per kid over the night; slow it down if the trays empty too fast.
  • Serve from the Igloo spigot. Top off with cool water for younger kids so it isn't scalding.

Food safety

  • Skewers point down when walking; no running near the fire.
  • A flaming marshmallow gets blown out and cooled before eating; it's hotter than it looks.
  • One adult stays at the fire the whole time.
  • Test the temperature before kids drink it.

Cleanup & leftovers

  • Wipe and wash the reusable skewers; count them back into the bin.
  • Bag opened grahams, chocolate and marshmallows; store them in a sealed bin or car overnight (critters).
  • Drown, stir and drown the fire before bed.
  • Rinse the Igloo while warm.

Planner notes

  • Kids go wild: planned ~3.5 s'mores per kid, ~1.5 per adult, per night.
  • Marshmallows carry a +50% burn/drop allowance; grahams +15% for breakage.
  • Vegan (gelatin-free) marshmallows go in a separate, labeled bag for vegetarian, halal and kosher eaters. Regular marshmallows are usually pork gelatin.
  • Bulk hot chocolate mix made in the big Igloo: heat water in pots, mix in the Igloo, serve from the spigot. Skip mixes with mini marshmallows: they usually contain pork gelatin.

Sun Breakfast

Breakfast bars + leftover fruit, coffee/tea, hot chocolate (no cooking) + Coffee + tea (packets) + Hot chocolate (bulk, in the big Igloo) · planned for about 83 people

  • Set-out (1 adult + scouts): bars, fruit, drinks station

  • large Igloo beverage cooler for hot chocolate

Prep & cooking (T = serving time)

T-30Heat water in pots (about 1 gallon per 16 cups). Whisk the mix into the big Igloo with the hot water (53 servings for this meal).
T-20Heat water in a large kettle or pot. Set out the coffee packets (65 packets), tea bags, creamer, sugar and sweetener with stir sticks.
T-10Set out breakfast bars (135 bars) and all the leftover fruit from yesterday.

Serving

  • About 1-2 bars each; fruit while it lasts (it's leftovers, not one per person).
  • Adults serve themselves; hot water stays on the adults' end of the table.
  • Serve from the Igloo spigot. Top off with cool water for younger kids so it isn't scalding.

Food safety

  • Keep hot water away from the kids' side of the table.
  • Hot water is adults only.
  • Test the temperature before kids drink it.

Cleanup & leftovers

  • Bag opened bars and remaining fruit to go home. Pack out all trash.
  • Rinse the Igloo while warm.
Leftovers may come to you: whole fruit from sat-lunch (Group 3 – Bears/Webelos). Use them if they're labeled, dated and cold. The plan doesn't count on them.

Planner notes

  • No cooking beyond hot water. Leftover fruit from earlier meals goes out here instead of buying more.
  • Choose nut-free bars without gelatin (check labels) while allergy status is unknown.
  • Coffee and tea are single-serve packets: just add hot water. Creamer, sugar and no-sugar sweetener on the side.
  • Bulk hot chocolate mix made in the big Igloo: heat water in pots, mix in the Igloo, serve from the spigot. Skip mixes with mini marshmallows: they usually contain pork gelatin.
