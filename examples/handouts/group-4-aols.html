Prep & cooking (T = serving time)

T-75Fill 2-3 large pots with water (about 5 quarts per pound of pasta, cooked in batches). Cover and bring to a boil; camp burners take 30-45 minutes.
T-60Handwash station up. Wipe down prep tables. Dice 2 onions for the meat sauce.
T-50VEG SAUCE FIRST: in its own pot, warm about 40% of the marinara (286.1 oz total) with Italian seasoning and garlic powder. Label the pot VEG and give it its own ladle.
T-45MEAT SAUCE: brown the ground turkey (7.2 lb) in a little oil with the diced onion, salt and pepper until there's no pink (165°F). Drain, add the rest of the marinara plus seasoning, and simmer 15 minutes. Label the pot MEAT.
T-30Salt the boiling water (about 14.6 oz across all batches). Cook the pasta (319.2 oz) in batches of about 2 lb per pot, 9-11 minutes. Drain, toss with a little oil, and hold covered.
T-15Fill the 5-gal coolers with cold water and ice. Stir in the mix: one canister makes about 34 qt, so about half a canister per 5-gal cooler (145 servings for this meal). Keep one cooler of plain water too.
T-10Salad: greens (47.2 oz) into bowls; dressing (26.1 oz) on the side. Parmesan (14.6 oz) in a bowl with a spoon.

Serving

plates/bowls (or mess kits) → pasta → VEG sauce (own ladle) → MEAT sauce (own ladle) → parmesan → salad + dressing → napkins

  • Line order: mess kits → pasta → VEG sauce → MEAT sauce → parmesan → salad. The veg sauce comes first so the meat ladle never touches it.
  • About 1 cup of pasta to start (1.5 for adults); seconds once everyone has gone through.
  • Keep both sauces on low heat, lids on.
  • Serve from the spigot into mess-kit cups.

Food safety

  • Ground turkey is cooked to 165°F with no pink.
  • Only adults handle boiling water and draining. Kids stay back from the burners.
  • Hot food stays hot; leftovers go into shallow containers and into the cooler within 2 hours.

Cleanup & leftovers

  • Let pasta water cool, strain out the food, and dispose of it per campground rules, away from water sources.
  • Scrape pots before washing. Sauce leftovers go home in containers; nothing is cooked Sunday.
  • Bag unused salad greens.
  • Rinse the coolers; drain away from the campsite.
Leftovers may come to you: deli turkey, sliced from sat-lunch (Group 3 – Bears/Webelos); lettuce, pre-washed from sat-lunch (Group 3 – Bears/Webelos); tomatoes from sat-lunch (Group 3 – Bears/Webelos); onions from sat-lunch (Group 3 – Bears/Webelos); cucumbers from sat-lunch (Group 3 – Bears/Webelos); bell peppers from sat-lunch (Group 3 – Bears/Webelos). Planner notes

  • Meat sauce uses ground turkey, not beef, so no-beef families are covered. It is not halal or kosher.
  • Vegetarian sauce is the planned main for vegetarian, halal and kosher eaters, plus ~25% of everyone else.
  • Separate ladles for each sauce; label the pots. Cook the veg sauce first.
  • Salad is intentionally small: pre-washed greens, one or two dressings.
  • Cooking staples: salt for the pasta water, oil for browning and tossing, Italian seasoning + garlic powder for both sauces.
  • Pre-sweetened powdered mix: one ~82 oz canister makes ~34 qt. Mix in the coolers with camp water; keep one cooler of plain water too.
