Sat Breakfast

Egg breakfast burritos + fruit + Coffee + tea (packets) + Hot chocolate (bulk, in the big Igloo) · planned for about 83 people

Special diets at this meal

Vegetarian Everything on the line is vegetarian (no meat at this meal).
Halal OK as served: no meat, and the cheese is kosher-certified (no animal rennet).
Kosher Sealed kosher meal (shared griddles aren't kosher). Hand it over unopened.

Crew (4 adults, 4 kids)

  • Griddle leads (2 adults): veggies, eggs, tortillas
  • Prep (1 adult): crack and whisk eggs, dice veggies
  • Line lead (1 adult) + kid helpers: fruit, setting out the line, serving cheese and salsa

Prep & cooking (T = serving time)

T-60Handwash station up (water jug, soap, paper towels). Wipe down prep tables. Set up griddles and burners; check fuel.
T-55Set the sealed kosher meals aside on a separate, labeled tray. They are not opened or heated on our griddles.
T-45Adults: dice 3 onions and 3 peppers on cutting boards. Kids: wash hands, glove up, and set out whole fruit (80 servings) in bins.
T-40Crack 266 eggs into large bowls, about 5 dozen per bowl. Whisk in a little salt and pepper. Keep the bowls in the cooler until they go on the griddle.
T-30Griddles to medium. Melt a little butter, then saute the peppers and onions 5 minutes until soft. Move them to a covered pan.
T-30Heat water in pots (about 1 gallon per 16 cups). Whisk the mix into the big Igloo with the hot water (51 servings for this meal).
T-25Scramble the eggs in batches (about 2 dozen per griddle at a time) with butter. Cook until firm with no runny parts. Hold in covered foil pans at the cool edge of the griddle.
T-20Heat water in a large kettle or pot. Set out the coffee packets (63 packets), tea bags, creamer, sugar and sweetener with stir sticks.
T-10Warm the tortillas (133 tortillas) 15-20 seconds per side; stack them in foil to stay soft.
T-5Set out the line: tortillas, eggs, peppers & onions, shredded cheese (70.4 oz), salsa (48.4 oz), hot sauce, fruit, napkins.

Serving

plates (or mess kits) → tortillas → eggs (spoon) → peppers & onions → cheese → salsa → hot sauce → fruit → napkins

  • One burrito each to start; seconds once everyone has gone through.
  • About 1/3 cup of eggs per burrito. Adults serve the eggs; kids can serve cheese and fruit.
  • Hot sauce stays at the end of the line, away from little hands.
  • Adults serve themselves; hot water stays on the adults' end of the table.
  • Serve from the Igloo spigot. Top off with cool water for younger kids so it isn't scalding.

Food safety

  • Raw eggs stay in the cooler until they hit the griddle. Separate spoons for raw and cooked eggs.
  • Cook eggs until firm (160°F). Cooked eggs are out no more than 2 hours (1 hour above 90°F).
  • Change gloves after handling raw eggs or shells.
  • Hot water is adults only.
  • Test the temperature before kids drink it.

Cleanup & leftovers

  • Scrape griddles while warm. Egg shells and food scraps go in the trash, not the fire (critters).
  • Leftover cooked eggs are thrown out, not saved.
  • Bag and label opened tortillas, cheese, and leftover diced veggies (see carry-forwards).
  • Rinse the Igloo while warm.
  • Save leftover onions for sat-lunch: bag, label, date, into the cooler.
  • Save leftover bell peppers for sat-dinner (use with the salad greens, pre-washed): bag, label, date, into the cooler.
  • Save leftover whole fruit for sat-lunch: bag, label, date, into the cooler.

Planner notes

  • "Lots of eggs" per the scoutmaster: planned 2.5 eggs per kid, 3.5 per adult.
  • Build-your-own: scrambled eggs, shredded cheese, peppers/onions, salsa. No meat, so it is pork- and beef-free and works for vegetarians.
  • Ask about egg allergies; a scout with one needs a separate plan.
  • Cooking staples: butter for the eggs, cooking spray for the griddle, salt, pepper, hot sauce on the line.
  • Coffee and tea are single-serve packets: just add hot water. Creamer, sugar and no-sugar sweetener on the side.
  • Bulk hot chocolate mix made in the big Igloo: heat water in pots, mix in the Igloo, serve from the spigot. Skip mixes with mini marshmallows: they usually contain pork gelatin.
  • 3 kosher eater(s) get a sealed certified meal; hot food from shared pots/griddles usually isn't kosher. Ask the families.
